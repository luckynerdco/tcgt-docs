## Overview

TCGTracking Stores lets you host your own public storefront directly through
TCGTracking — completely free for all TCGTracking users. Whether you are an
individual seller, run a local game store, or set up at card shows, Stores gives you
a fully customizable space to sell your cards online.

Stores are powered by Stripe for payments, meaning checkout is fast, secure, and
trusted by buyers. Once your store is live, anyone can browse your inventory and
purchase directly from you.

<Aside type="tip">
TCGTracking Stores are completely free to set up and use. You only need a TCGTracking
account and a Stripe account to get started.
</Aside>

## Seller responsibilities

As a store owner, you are responsible for managing your own storefront. TCGTracking
provides the platform — the rest is up to you.

- You are responsible for collecting and remitting any applicable sales taxes in your region.
- You are responsible for the day-to-day management and operation of your store.
- Stripe's standard processing fees apply to all transactions.

<Aside type="danger">
Tax laws vary by location. Make sure you understand your local tax obligations before
opening your store. TCGT.Help and TCGTracking do not provide tax advice.
</Aside>

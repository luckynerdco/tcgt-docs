## How a contribution becomes a guide

1. You edit a page or add a new one (in your browser or locally).
2. You open a **pull request** — a proposal of your changes.
3. A maintainer reviews it, suggests tweaks if needed, and approves.
4. Once merged, it builds and goes live on TCGT.Help automatically.

<Aside type="tip" title="Easiest place to start">
Any page marked **"Coming soon"** is an open invitation — pick one you know well
and write it. The structure and styling are already in place; you just add the words.
</Aside>

## What makes a good guide

Write the way you'd explain it to a fellow seller at the next table: practical,
step-by-step, and honest about the gotchas. Keep it focused on getting the task
done. The [Style Guide](/contribute/style-guide/) covers the formatting basics.

<Aside type="note">
TCGT.Help is an independent, community-run site and is not affiliated with
TCGTracking. Please only share workflows and information you're comfortable
making public.
</Aside>

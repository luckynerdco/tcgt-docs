A few light conventions keep guides consistent and easy to read. None of this is
strict — a maintainer will help tidy formatting during review.

## Voice

Write like one seller helping another: practical, direct, and friendly. Prefer
"you" and active steps ("Click **Import**") over passive description. Call out
gotchas honestly.

## Structure

Use `##` headings to break a guide into clear sections. Keep paragraphs short.
Lead with the task, then the details.

## Handy components

These are available in any `.mdx` guide:

- **Steps** — for numbered, ordered instructions.
- **Asides** — callout boxes for tips and warnings:

<Aside type="tip">Use a tip for a helpful shortcut.</Aside>
<Aside type="caution">Use caution for something that can go wrong.</Aside>

## Steps example

<Steps>

1. Describe the first action in bold, then explain it.

2. Keep each step to a single clear action.

</Steps>

<Aside type="note">
When in doubt, look at a finished guide like **Importing Orders** and follow the
same patterns. And don't sweat the details — just get the workflow written down,
and we'll polish formatting together in review.
</Aside>

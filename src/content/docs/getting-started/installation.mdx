## First-time setup

<Steps>

1. **Create your account** at [tcgt.help](https://tcgt.help) and verify your email.

2. **Connect a carrier.** Under **Settings → Carriers**, add your EasyPost key:

   ```bash
   # Paste your key when prompted, or set it as an environment variable
   export EASYPOST_API_KEY="EZAK_your_key_here"
   ```

3. **Print a test label** to confirm your printer and tracking are wired up.

</Steps>

:::caution
Keep your EasyPost key private — treat it like a password. If it leaks, rotate it
from the EasyPost dashboard immediately.
:::

Learn how to print trackable envelopes, carrier labels, and process orders for shipment.

## Overview

TCGTracking gives you two ways to ship your orders. The first is printing
trackable envelopes with USPS Intelligent Mail Barcodes, which is completely free
and provides real-time tracking. The second is purchasing carrier labels through
EasyPost for orders that need full postage.

Trackable envelopes are free and work great for standard card orders. Carrier
labels are needed when you want to purchase postage directly through TCGTracking.

## Printing trackable envelopes

Trackable envelopes use USPS Intelligent Mail Barcodes to provide real-time
tracking at no cost. All you need is a standard home printer.

<Steps>

1. **Import your orders using Quick Create**

2. **Select the orders you want to ship using the checkboxes**

3. **Click Print Envelopes**

4. **Choose your envelope size**

5. **TCGTracking will generate a PDF with all your envelopes**

6. **Print the PDF on your home printer**

7. **Fold, seal, and drop in the mail**

</Steps>

<Aside type="danger" title="Always print at 100% scale">
Do not use "fit to page" or "scale to fit" options. Resizing the barcode will
cause tracking to fail.
</Aside>

## Batch printing

Batch printing lets you process up to 100 orders at once, generating a single PDF
with all your envelopes ready to print in one go.

<Steps>

1. **Import all your orders for the session**

2. **Click the checkbox at the top to select all orders**

3. **Click Batch Print**

4. **Review the order summary and confirm**

5. **Print the generated PDF**

</Steps>

<Aside type="tip">
Many sellers do one or two batch print sessions per day. Import all your orders,
batch print, and ship them all at once to save time.
</Aside>

## Carrier labels

If you need to purchase postage, you can print USPS carrier labels directly
through TCGTracking using the EasyPost integration.

<Steps>

1. **Make sure you have EasyPost connected**

   And your wallet funded — see the [EasyPost Integration](/printing-shipping/easypost-integration/) guide.

2. **Select the orders you want to ship**

3. **Click Purchase Labels**

4. **Select your mail class**

   Ground Advantage, Priority, etc.

5. **Confirm the purchase and print your labels**

</Steps>

## Shipping to military addresses

TCGTracking supports shipping to APO, FPO, and DPO military addresses through
EasyPost with full customs support.

<Steps>

1. **Make sure EasyPost is connected to your account**

2. **Enter the military address as normal during order creation**

3. **TCGTracking will automatically detect the military address type**

4. **A customs form (CN22 or commercial invoice) will be generated automatically**

5. **Print the label and customs form together and attach to your package**

</Steps>

<Aside type="caution">
Military address shipments require a carrier label purchased through EasyPost.
Free trackable envelopes cannot be used for APO/FPO/DPO addresses.
</Aside>

## Printing tips

<Steps>

1. **Always print at 100% scale**

   Never use "fit to page." The barcode must be printed at exact size for USPS
   scanners to read it correctly.

2. **Use good quality paper**

   Standard 20lb copy paper works fine. Avoid very thin paper, as barcodes may
   bleed through.

3. **Check ink levels before a big batch**

   A faded barcode may not scan correctly. Make sure your printer has sufficient
   ink before printing a large batch.

4. **Verify addresses before printing**

   TCGTracking flags potential address issues automatically. Always resolve any
   warnings before printing.

5. **Drop off at the post office or use a blue box**

   For trackable envelopes, drop them at a USPS collection point to ensure they
   get scanned promptly.

</Steps>

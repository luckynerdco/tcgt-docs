## General

<details>
<summary>What is TCGTracking?</summary>

TCGTracking is a free shipping and order management platform built specifically for
trading card game sellers. It provides real-time USPS tracking for envelopes and
supports sellers across multiple marketplaces including TCGPlayer, Manapool,
Cardsphere, eBay, Amazon, and Shopify.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Is TCGTracking free to use?</summary>

Yes! TCGTracking is completely free to use. The only cost is PIP insurance, which is
optional and only charged when you use it. There is also a **Beta Supporter plan at
$9.99/month** which includes lower insurance rates and early access to new features.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Do I need a credit card to sign up?</summary>

No. You can create a free account without a credit card.

</details>

<details>
<summary>What marketplaces does TCGTracking support?</summary>

TCGTracking supports importing orders from TCGPlayer, Manapool, Cardsphere, eBay,
Amazon, Shopify, and CSV file upload.

</details>

<details>
<summary>What does the repeat icon 🔁 mean?</summary>

The repeat icon means the buyer has purchased from you before. It is a repeat-customer
indicator to help you identify your returning buyers at a glance.

</details>

## Orders & importing

<details>
<summary>How do I import orders from TCGPlayer?</summary>

Log into your TCGTracking dashboard, go to Quick Create, and select TCGPlayer as your
import source. You can import orders directly via API sync or by uploading a CSV export
from TCGPlayer. See [Importing Orders](/order-management/importing-orders/) for the full
walkthrough.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I enter orders manually?</summary>

Yes. Use the Quick Create tool on your dashboard to manually enter individual orders.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I bulk process orders?</summary>

Yes. TCGTracking supports batch processing of up to 100 orders at once.

</details>

## Printing & shipping

<details>
<summary>How do I print a trackable envelope?</summary>

After importing your orders, select the orders you want to ship and click Print
Envelopes. TCGTracking will generate a PDF with USPS Intelligent Mail Barcodes that you
print at home on any standard printer. See [Printing](/printing-shipping/printing/) for
details.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Do I need a special printer?</summary>

No. Any standard home printer will work for printing trackable envelopes.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I buy postage through TCGTracking?</summary>

Yes. Through the EasyPost integration you can purchase USPS e-stamps and Ground
Advantage carrier labels directly from your dashboard.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Does TCGTracking support shipping to military addresses?</summary>

Yes. TCGTracking supports shipping to APO, FPO, and DPO military addresses with full
customs support through EasyPost.

</details>

## Tracking

<details>
<summary>How does tracking work?</summary>

TCGTracking uses USPS Informed Visibility to provide real-time tracking updates as your
envelope moves through the postal network. Tracking is 100% free. See
[IMB Tracking](/printing-shipping/imb-tracking/) for the full explanation.

</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I track a package?</summary>

You can track by order number or tracking number from your dashboard. There is also a
public tracking page at [tcgtracking.com/track.php](https://tcgtracking.com/track.php).

</details>

<details>
<summary>How accurate is the tracking?</summary>

TCGTracking provides GPS-accurate scan events with delivery date predictions as your
package moves through USPS facilities.

</details>

## PIP insurance

<details>
<summary>What is PIP insurance?</summary>

PIP stands for Parcel Insurance Plan. It is optional shipment insurance that covers your
envelopes up to $50 per shipment in case of loss or damage. See
[PIP Insurance](/printing-shipping/pip-insurance/) for full details.

</details>

<details>
<summary>How much does PIP insurance cost?</summary>

On the free plan the rate is **$0.17 minimum or 0.85% of declared value**, whichever is
greater. Beta Supporter plan members pay **$0.13 minimum or 0.70%** of declared value.

</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I file a claim?</summary>

Claims can be filed directly through your TCGTracking dashboard. The process is
straightforward and proof-of-delivery tracking is included automatically.

</details>

## Account

<details>
<summary>How do I create an account?</summary>

Visit [tcgtracking.com](https://tcgtracking.com) and click Create Free Account. No
credit card required.

</details>

<details>
<summary>What is the Beta Supporter plan?</summary>

The Beta Supporter plan is $9.99/month and includes lower PIP insurance rates, early
access to new features, priority support, and a beta supporter badge.

</details>

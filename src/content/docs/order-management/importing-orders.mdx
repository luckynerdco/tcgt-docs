import { Steps } from '@astrojs/starlight/components';

TCGTracking pulls orders from your connected marketplaces so you can print and
ship from one place instead of jumping between tabs.

<Steps>

1. Go to **Orders → Import**.

2. Choose a source — **TCGplayer**, **ManaPool**, or **eBay**.

3. Pick a date range and click **Import**. New orders land in your **Open** queue.

</Steps>

:::tip
Imports are incremental — running the same import again only pulls orders you
haven't seen yet, so it's safe to re-run whenever.
:::

Once orders are in, head to [Printing & Shipping](/printing-shipping/printing/)
to print labels and get them out the door.

Sync your TCGPlayer orders, inventory, and pricing directly to your TCGTracking
dashboard.

## Overview

The TCGTracking Bridge is a Chrome browser extension that works like you logging into
your TCGPlayer seller portal. It reads the same information your browser sees when you
visit TCGPlayer — orders, inventory, pricing — and relays it to your TCGTracking
dashboard so you can manage everything in one place.

### How it works

- After you sign into TCGPlayer in your browser, the extension reads information from your seller portal exactly as if you were viewing those pages yourself.
- All requests are made by your device, your IP address, and your browser — identical to normal browsing.
- TCGTracking does not store or have access to your OAuth tokens, session cookies, or any secret keys.
- The extension communicates directly between your browser and TCGPlayer — your credentials are never transmitted.

### What it does

- View and import orders from your TCGPlayer seller portal.
- Push inventory quantities and pricing directly to TCGPlayer by hitting "Sync Now" on the inventory screen or "Sync to TCGPlayer" via the sidebar.
- Read your seller ID and account details.
- Auto-detects if you need to log into TCGPlayer first.
- All communication stays between your browser and TCGPlayer.

<Aside type="note">
You must be logged into TCGPlayer in Chrome for the extension to work. If your session
expires, the extension will auto-detect this and prompt you to log in again.
</Aside>

## Installing the extension

The TCGTracking Bridge is installed as an unpacked Chrome extension. This takes about
two minutes.

<Steps>

1. **Download the extension ZIP**

   Download the Bridge Extension ZIP file from the link at the bottom of this page.

2. **Extract the ZIP to a folder**

   Extract the contents to a permanent folder on your computer — for example, create a folder called `TCGTracking-Bridge` on your Desktop.

3. **Open Chrome Extensions and enable Developer Mode**

   Open Chrome and type `chrome://extensions` in the address bar. Toggle Developer mode ON using the switch in the top-right corner.

4. **Load the unpacked extension**

   Click Load unpacked and select the folder you extracted in step 2. The TCGTracking Bridge extension will appear in your extensions list.

</Steps>

<Aside type="danger" title="Don't delete the folder">
Do not delete the extracted folder after loading. Chrome reads from it directly. If
you move or delete it, the extension will break and you will need to reload it.
</Aside>

## Importing inventory from TCGPlayer

Once the Bridge extension is installed and you are logged into TCGPlayer in Chrome,
you can bulk import your live inventory directly into TCGTracking in just a few clicks
— no file export or mapping step needed.

<Steps>

1. **Go to the Inventory tab**

   Navigate to the [Inventory tab](https://tcgtracking.com/dashboard.php?tab=inventory) in your TCGTracking dashboard.

2. **Open Bulk Import**

   Click Bulk Tools, then select Bulk Import / Update.

3. **Select From TCGPlayer (Bridge)**

   Click the From TCGPlayer (Bridge) tab. If the extension is installed and you are signed in, you will see a green message: "TCGPlayer Bridge detected & signed in."

4. **Fetch your inventory**

   Click Fetch My Inventory. Your live inventory will load straight into the review screen with no mapping step required.

</Steps>

<Aside type="tip">
If the Bridge is not installed or you are not signed into TCGPlayer, TCGTracking will
fall back to your most recent Flow desktop-agent snapshot automatically.
</Aside>

## Importing orders from TCGPlayer

Once the Bridge extension is installed and you are logged into TCGPlayer in Chrome,
you can pull your open orders directly into TCGTracking without exporting any files.

<Steps>

1. **Go to the Orders tab**

   Navigate to the [Orders tab](https://tcgtracking.com/dashboard.php?tab=orders&orders_tab=open) in your TCGTracking dashboard.

2. **Open the TCGPlayer Bridge tab**

   Click the [TCGPlayer Bridge](https://tcgtracking.com/dashboard.php?tab=orders&orders_tab=tcgbridge&page=1) tab. Your open TCGPlayer orders will load automatically.

3. **Import your orders**

   Review the orders and click Import. The orders will be pulled into TCGTracking and ready to process.

4. **Return to Open Orders**

   Head back to your Open Orders tab and continue with your normal shipping workflow — print envelopes, add insurance, and ship.

</Steps>

<Aside type="note">
Make sure you are logged into TCGPlayer in Chrome before using the Bridge tab. If your
session has expired, TCGTracking will prompt you to log back in.
</Aside>

## Important notes

- This extension is **not affiliated with, endorsed by, or associated with TCGPlayer**.
- It reads publicly available seller portal data using your authenticated browser session.
- API calls go directly from your browser to TCGPlayer — if you choose to import or sync data, it is processed at your request.
- You must be logged into TCGPlayer in Chrome for the extension to work.

<Aside type="note">
This extension only works in Chrome. Other browsers such as Firefox, Safari, and Edge
are not currently supported.
</Aside>

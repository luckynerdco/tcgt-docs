A Chrome extension that adds quality-of-life tools and workflow shortcuts on top of
your TCGTracking dashboard.

## Overview

TCGTracking Utilities is a free Chrome extension built by **Kraken**, a member of the
TCGTracking community, that layers extra tools and shortcuts directly onto your
dashboard to speed up order management and fulfillment. It runs entirely in your
browser alongside TCGTracking — no separate account or login required.

<Aside type="tip">
Built for sellers who process large order volumes and want faster, more accurate
order handling.
</Aside>

<Aside type="note">
This is a community-built tool, not an official TCGTracking product. It is listed
here because it's widely used and trusted within the seller community, but it is
developed and maintained independently.
</Aside>

## Features

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Quick Marketplace Links">Open the matching marketplace order directly from TCGTracking in one click.</Card>
  <Card title="PIP Claim Autofill">Automatically fills out PIP insurance claim forms to cut down on manual data entry.</Card>
  <Card title="Duplicate Order Detection">Flags potential duplicate orders before you process and ship them.</Card>
  <Card title="Custom Customer Flags">Flag orders automatically based on customer name, address, city, state, or ZIP code.</Card>
  <Card title="Google Maps Integration">Open any shipping address in Google Maps for quick validation before you ship.</Card>
  <Card title="Workflow Enhancements">Additional shortcuts and small utilities aimed at reducing errors and saving clicks.</Card>
</CardGrid>

## Installing the extension

<Steps>

1. **Open the Chrome Web Store listing**

   Go to the [TCGTracking Utilities listing](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/tcgtracking-utilities/djllhfipchmehchmanpnpnjmahpfeafd).

2. **Click Add to Chrome**

   Confirm the permissions prompt to finish installing.

3. **Pin the extension**

   Click the puzzle-piece icon in your Chrome toolbar and pin TCGTracking Utilities for quick access.

4. **Log into TCGTracking as normal**

   The extension activates automatically on your TCGTracking dashboard — no separate setup needed.

</Steps>

<Aside type="caution">
This extension only works in Chrome (and other Chromium-based browsers like Edge or
Brave). It is not available for Firefox or Safari.
</Aside>

## Privacy

The developer has disclosed that this extension does not collect or sell your data,
and does not use your data for anything unrelated to the extension's core
functionality.

Use TCGTracking to manage your card inventory and sync stock across TCGPlayer and Manapool.

## Overview

TCGTracking includes a built-in inventory management system that lets you organize
your card stock into physical locations and keep your listings in sync across
multiple marketplaces. Instead of manually updating stock on each platform,
TCGTracking handles it for you automatically.

<Aside type="tip">
Inventory management works alongside your existing order workflow. When an order is
fulfilled, stock levels update automatically across your connected marketplaces.
</Aside>

### Supported marketplaces

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="TCGPlayer">Sync inventory directly with your TCGPlayer seller account.</Card>
  <Card title="Manapool">Sync inventory directly with your Manapool seller account.</Card>
</CardGrid>

## Enabling inventory management

Inventory management is not enabled by default. You need to turn it on in your
settings before you can start using it.

<Steps>

1. **Go to your TCGTracking dashboard**

2. **Navigate to Inventory in the main navigation**

3. **Click the Settings subtab**

4. **Toggle Inventory Management to on**

5. **Save your settings**

   Then head to [Inventory Settings](https://tcgtracking.com/dashboard.php?tab=inventory&subtab=inventory&section=settings).

</Steps>

<Aside type="note">
Once enabled, you will see new tabs appear in the Inventory section including
Locations, Stock, and Sync settings.
</Aside>

## Syncing with marketplaces

TCGTracking lets you control exactly which marketplaces each location syncs with.
This is useful if you keep separate inventory for different platforms. For example:

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="A-1-A">Manapool only</Card>
  <Card title="A-2-A">TCGPlayer only</Card>
  <Card title="A-3-A">Both platforms</Card>
</CardGrid>

<Aside type="tip">
You can toggle sync on or off per platform for each individual location. This gives
you full control over where your stock is listed without managing it manually on
each site.
</Aside>
